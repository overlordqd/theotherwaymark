# The Other Waymarks: people

The people you can meet in the valley. Dialogue is written against this file, so keep it current when a story changes.

## How meeting people works

- **You do not know anyone's name until they tell you.** Before that, the journal and the conversation box use what you can see: "woman in a yellow jacket", "man with a radio mast on his pack". A name arrives in conversation, from someone else ("that was Howard, he keeps the pass hut"), or never.
- Trail names are common among long-distance walkers. Some people tell you their trail name first, and their real name only later, if at all.
- Each conversation offers two or three questions. Each question opens a different part of their story; you cannot ask everything at one meeting. People are met again over several days, in different places, and remember what you talked about.
- Choosing a question continues the conversation at once. Lines move on when you have read them, or on a click, Enter or Space.
- Everything said goes into the journal in the hiker's voice, including what they would not answer.

## The backbone (for writers, never told outright)

A year ago, in the same last week of August, a field recordist called **Euwin** walked into this valley and did not come back. Since then:
- faded blue waymarks have appeared, leaving the official red-and-white route and climbing toward the **high field** below the pass;
- cassette tapes of Euwin's recordings turn up along that blue route;
- on certain nights a waltz is heard in the trees and on the radio, always at 21:47;
- the sky does things it should not: an aurora this far south, stars a few degrees out of place;
- the line of blue marks points at the notch in the ridge where the sun rises on **24 August**, the date of the uncanny night in the showreel.

Nobody in the valley holds the whole picture. Each person below holds a piece, and some of them are part of the strangeness themselves. The game never says what happened to Euwin, what paints the marks or what walks around the tent. Possible readings (a thin place where the sky comes close, a loop in time, a way out of the world) are all left open.

## Thalia

- **First seen as:** a woman in a yellow jacket and a brimmed hat, coming down from the pass. Some hikers call her **Moth**, because she walks at dusk.
- **Name:** she tells you at the end of the first meeting.
- **Background:** late fifties, a retired land surveyor from the coast. She walks to her sister's on the far side of the pass every August, and has done so for twenty years. This year she turned back.
- **Wants:** to reach her sister without going past the hut.
- **Topics:** where she started (three weeks on foot from the coast) · why she turned back (the pass hut is boarded up, and someone is sleeping in it anyway) · the blue marks ("Don't follow them after dark").
- **Thread:** last August she walked two days with a quiet Welsh man who recorded birdsong on cassettes. She never heard what became of him. She recognises his voice if you play her one of the tapes.
- **Over the days:** met again in a village, waiting for a bus that does not come up the valley in August. Later she decides to cross the pass with you, if you ask.
- **Never explained:** her old survey map of the valley shows a path where the blue marks run. It is dated 1938.

## Cindy

- **First seen as:** the girl at the bakery in the first village, flour on her apron, up before dawn.
- **Name:** on her name badge, if you go in; or the innkeeper shouts it.
- **Background:** nineteen, the baker's daughter, saving to leave the valley for art school. She draws every walker who buys bread, in a sketchbook kept under the counter.
- **Wants:** to get out; and, secretly, to know what happened to the man she drew last August.
- **Topics:** the weather today (she reads the sky better than the radio) · the walkers who passed this week · her sketchbook.
- **Thread:** she recognises people from photos. Show her the SD card's pictures and she names who took them, and the hiker in the early shots. Her sketch from last August shows Euwin, asking her which way was "the other path". He bought two loaves for one person.
- **Over the days:** if you come back through her village, she has drawn you, though she never saw you buy bread.
- **Never explained:** that drawing is dated the day before you arrived.

## Laure

- **First seen as:** a woman with a telescope case on her back, walking against the flow at dusk.
- **Name:** she introduces herself straight away and shakes your hand. She is the only one who does.
- **Background:** forties, an astronomer from Liège who rents the old shepherd's shelter on the ridge every summer for its dark sky. Precise, dry, funny, and quietly shaken this year.
- **Wants:** a second observation. One night is an anomaly; two is data.
- **Topics:** the stars (she shows you how to find the galaxy band) · the aurora ("At this latitude? No.") · her logbook.
- **Thread:** her logbook records the same aurora a year ago, the same week, the night Euwin vanished. On some nights she measures stars a few degrees from where they should be, but only from the high field.
- **Over the days:** asks you to note the time of anything strange you see in the sky. Your journal entries can fill her gaps.
- **Never explained:** her photographs of the aurora show it from above.

## Donna

- **First seen as:** an older woman building a cairn by the trail, stone by careful stone.
- **Name:** she tells you once you help her place a stone.
- **Background:** late sixties, widowed this spring. She and her husband walked this valley every year for forty years. She is walking it alone for the first time, building a small cairn at each of their old resting places.
- **Wants:** to finish the walk they never finished: the pass, together.
- **Topics:** her husband (warm, funny stories, and one sad one) · the cairns · whether she will make the pass alone.
- **Thread:** every cairn she builds is found rebuilt in the morning, a little further up, toward the blue route. She is not frightened: "He always knew the way better than me."
- **Over the days:** her cairns appear along your route, then along the blue route. One night you see her headlamp high on the blue route, where no one should be.
- **Never explained:** whether she reaches the pass. Her last cairn stands on the high field.

## Jack

- **First seen as:** a man in running shorts, overtaking you uphill at speed, waving.
- **Name:** he shouts it over his shoulder the first time, then reminds you every time ("Jack! Still me!").
- **Background:** thirties, a cheerful trail runner from the city, attempting a fastest-known-time on the valley loop. Talks a lot, notices little.
- **Wants:** the record, and someone to tell about it.
- **Topics:** his time ("Day three of my run, on pace") · his kit · whether you have seen a runner ahead (he is sure someone is ahead of him).
- **Thread:** he overtakes you again and again, including after you saw him running the other way. He always says it is day three of his run.
- **Over the days:** comic relief at first. Later he asks you, a little lost, what day it is.
- **Never explained:** his watch shows the same date every time.

## Reilly

- **First seen as:** a young man with a pot of paint and a brush, freshening a red-and-white waymark.
- **Name:** he tells you while complaining about the paint.
- **Background:** twenty-four, a volunteer trail warden for the valley association. Practical, proud of his marks, sceptical of everything.
- **Wants:** to find whoever keeps painting over his marks, and to stop them.
- **Topics:** the trail and its marks (how to read them, how far the next hut is) · the painted-over marks ("kids from town") · the paint itself.
- **Thread:** the blue paint is not sold anywhere he knows. He has a sample analysed: the pigment matches the old marks on the 1938 map in the inn.
- **Over the days:** he starts walking the blue route at night to catch whoever does it. He asks you to come along.
- **Never explained:** the morning after, his own brush is blue.

## Howard

- **First seen as:** an old man in a wool coat on the bench outside the boarded-up pass hut.
- **Name:** others tell you ("That's Howard, he keeps the hut"). He never says it himself.
- **Background:** seventies, hut warden at the pass for fifty summers, born in the valley. Gruff, kind when it matters, full of old stories he half believes.
- **Wants:** to keep people off the high field this week, without having to say why.
- **Topics:** the hut (closed "because of the noises") · the old way ("Before the red and white there was only the blue") · the week ("Come back in September").
- **Thread:** he is the one sleeping in the closed hut, keeping watch. He knew Euwin, and he was the last to see him, walking up into the dark with his recorder running.
- **Over the days:** opens up if you bring him something from the blue route. Tells the valley's story of the "other waymarks": they lead to where the sky comes down to meet the ground, once a year.
- **Never explained:** he says he has been up there once, as a boy, and that it is the most beautiful thing he has ever seen.

## Randall

- **First seen as:** a man camped with a tall antenna mast, headphones on, by the lake.
- **Name:** in his call sign, on a card he hands you; then he says it.
- **Background:** sixties, a retired radio engineer and ham operator who comes for the radio silence of the valley. Methodical, kind, lonely.
- **Wants:** to identify a signal.
- **Topics:** radio (he lets you listen) · the signal · his late wife, who loved waltzes.
- **Thread:** at 21:47 on certain nights he picks up a scratchy waltz on a frequency nobody broadcasts on. It skips in the same place every time. It is the record you heard in the trees.
- **Over the days:** he records it and plays it backwards for you. Under the music there is birdsong, recorded on a cassette.
- **Never explained:** where it comes from. He stops wanting to know.

## Vikky

- **First seen as:** a woman filming herself on a phone at a viewpoint, a drone buzzing overhead.
- **Name:** she says it to camera: "Hi everyone, Vikky here."
- **Background:** late twenties, a travel vlogger doing the valley for her channel. Upbeat, generous, sharper than she lets on.
- **Wants:** the shot nobody else has; secretly, to be taken seriously.
- **Topics:** her channel (she asks to film you) · the views · what her drone saw last night.
- **Thread:** her night footage from the drone shows small lights moving in a ring on the high field. She did not see them while flying.
- **Over the days:** she posts the clip; it gets no views at all, as if nobody can see it. She asks you if you can.
- **Never explained:** in the corner of one clip there is a hiker with your jacket and pack.

## Euwin

- **First seen as:** a quiet man by the creek at dusk, holding a microphone toward the water, a cassette recorder on his hip.
- **Name:** he tells you softly, and spells it, because people get it wrong.
- **Background:** thirties, a Welsh field recordist making an album of the valley's sounds. Gentle, curious, completely at ease.
- **Wants:** one more recording: "The sound the high field makes at night."
- **Topics:** what he is recording (he lets you listen through his headphones) · the birds (he knows every call) · the blue marks ("They're not for everyone. They might be for you.").
- **Thread:** he is the walker who vanished a year ago. You meet him once, at dusk, and he seems entirely real. He gives you a tape.
- **Over the days:** Victor's posters, Cindy's sketch and Howard's story tell you who he was. His tapes turn up along the blue route; the last one has your voice on it.
- **Never explained:** anything about him.

## Victor

- **First seen as:** a man taping a poster to a signpost in the rain.
- **Name:** he introduces himself formally, with a handshake: "Victor Price."
- **Background:** forties, an accountant from Cardiff, Euwin's older brother. Came back for the anniversary to put up posters and walk the route his brother walked.
- **Wants:** an answer, even a bad one.
- **Topics:** his brother (photos on his phone) · what the police said ("Lost. Fell. Gone.") · whether you have seen anything.
- **Thread:** the poster carries Euwin's photo. If you met Euwin, you recognise him, and you have to decide whether to say so.
- **Over the days:** if you tell him, he asks you to show him where. If you give him the tape, he listens alone, and thanks you the next morning.
- **Never explained:** he says Euwin phoned him from the valley last night, a year late.

## Joana

- **First seen as:** a woman with a camera, crouched in the grass photographing something you cannot see.
- **Name:** she tells you once she notices you are holding her SD card. She knew it was hers before you showed it.
- **Background:** thirties, a Portuguese landscape photographer working on a series about "places where the light is wrong". Intense, warm once she trusts you.
- **Wants:** her card back, but not to look at it.
- **Topics:** her series · how she lost the card (in the rain on the ridge, a week ago) · the photos on it.
- **Thread:** the SD card you found is hers. She took the village and the blue rock. She has no memory of the flash photos at midnight, or of the photo of your tent, taken a week after she lost the card.
- **Over the days:** she photographs you, "for the series". The next day she shows you the frame: you are standing on the high field.
- **Never explained:** she never looks at the last photo on the card.

## Lim

- **First seen as:** a person kneeling by a lichen-covered boulder with a notebook and a compass, measuring.
- **Name:** they tell you their name and their field in one breath: "Lim, geologist, sorry, am I in your way?"
- **Background:** thirty, a Dutch-Korean geologist mapping old cup-marked stones in the valley for a university survey. Methodical, funny, allergic to anything they cannot measure, and slowly losing that fight.
- **Wants:** to finish the survey before the weather turns.
- **Topics:** the stones (some are carved, thousands of years old) · the blue marks ("Have you noticed they're all on a straight line?") · the stars.
- **Thread:** the blue marks follow a straight line from the lake to the notch in the ridge where the sun rises on 24 August. So do the old carved stones.
- **Over the days:** compares notes with Laure. Asks you to take a bearing on the blue marks with your compass. The bearing is different every night.
- **Never explained:** Lim's measurements of the line are exact, and the line moves.

## A note on the SD card thread

The SD card thread is inspired by the feeling of real found-camera cases, but Joana, Euwin and Victor are fictional and their stories are our own. Never use real names, real photos or details of real disappearances.
